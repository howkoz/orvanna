# Orvanna, company profile

> As of 2026-08-13. Canonical home: `ORVANNA\LIBRARY\company\`.

## What Orvanna is

Orvanna is Howard Koziara's personal venture brand. Domain: orvanna.io. Everything
under the name is built on Howard's own time, accounts, and equipment, in its own
private repository and its own cloud organization, fully separate from any employer
material (the separation is documented and was executed 2026-08-13).

## The two product lines

1. **The Platform (flagship, paused before build phase):** a universal payment
   orchestration product concept: routing, vault, and 3-D Secure as a neutral layer.
   Fourteen system-design models completed; build phase awaits Howard's go.
2. **The MLM Pilot (active):** a working direct-selling (multi-level marketing, MLM)
   prototype: a fictional marketplace where members subscribe to digital AI agents
   (domain agents $100 per month at 100 Personal Volume, support agents $50 at 50),
   with a five-level compensation plan, monthly commission runs producing locked
   auditable statements, 1,000 synthetic members, and a public demo portal headed to
   orvanna.io. Compensation rules: the booklet in `MLM-PILOT\docs\` is the
   member-facing truth; the specification in the same folder is the engine's truth;
   both carry the same numbers.

## How the company works (the operating system)

- One owner (Howard) decides; a six-agent specialist team builds and checks (see
  `TEAM-PROFILE.md`).
- Every build phase ends at TWO independent gates: a verifier that recomputes the
  math from scratch and a quality assurance agent that traces every promise to
  evidence. Builders never grade their own work. Both gates must PASS.
- Everything is versioned in git, pushed to the private howkoz/orvanna repository.
- Determinism is a house value: seeds, runs, and statements reproduce byte for byte.

## The fence (ownership guardrails)

Zero employer data, documents, internal names, or proprietary numbers anywhere in the
Orvanna tree. Public industry mechanics may inform designs only in generic renamed
form. Personal sessions run from the ORVANNA folder. Nothing is ever deleted; moves
leave pointer notes.
