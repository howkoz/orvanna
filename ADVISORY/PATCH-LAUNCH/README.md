# Patch Launch Playbook (advisory, 2026-09-08)

> Personal advisory work. Howard was asked by a founding group with a nicotine patch
> product for a high-level plan on creating and running a multi-level marketing (MLM)
> company in the US, start to finish, presented from twenty years of field experience.
> Nothing here is employer material; the group is unrelated to Howard's job.

## The document

`patch-launch-playbook.html`: a self-contained speaking guide for a two-hour founder
briefing. Open it in any browser; no server, no network. Twelve sections:

| # | Section | What it settles |
|---|---|---|
| 00 | How to open the room | The first two minutes, and the sentence that frames everything after |
| 01 | The two things that matter | Products and the comp plan are the business; everything else is plumbing |
| 02 | Reality check first: nicotine is regulated three ways | A patch is a cessation drug, a tobacco product, or a supplement it cannot be; the founders must know which, in writing |
| 03 | Product: does a patch fit the model? | The consumable test, the $14 to $60 to $75 to 60 PV to 48 CV pricing flow |
| 04 | Compensation plan | The five plan shapes, the payout budget, what the regulator tests, whose decision the plan is, the plan design workshop |
| 05 | Legal, corporate and compliance | Entity, policies, income disclosure, the six state MLM registration states |
| 06 | Operations and technology | Platform, payments, payouts, fulfilment |
| 07 | Building the field | The first twenty, onboarding, claims control |
| 08 | Money: what it costs and where it goes | Capital in ranges, where the first eighteen months of spend goes |
| 09 | Roadmap, 0 to 18 months | Five phases with gates |
| 10 | Deal-breakers, said before they ask | What kills companies in this category |
| 11 | Closing the conversation | What Howard needs from them, what he would do next |

Green "Say this" boxes are words to use in the room. "The experience line" boxes are
the twenty-years perspective, one per section. The orange boxes are warnings.

## The stance on the comp plan (section 04)

The founders choose the plan. Howard teaches the five shapes, recommends a starting
point (unilevel or a lean unilevel hybrid with a hard per-order ceiling), runs a
two-to-three-hour plan design workshop where they answer six questions, and then
models the one or two candidates they leave with against their real costs. The
decision is theirs because the plan is the promise they make to their field.
