## Defects

### MEDIUM

- **M1. The staff billing console, a promised S1 deliverable, is absent from
  this commit.** Spec section 9A.3: "The console core lands in Phase S1."
  Deferred with argument ("engine first, console second") in the proof
  document, so it is a disclosed scope gap, not a silent one, but Phase S1
  cannot close until the console (page, Edge Functions, schedule control,
  dry-run preview, run history drill-down, retry queue, attention queue,
  forecast, subscription management) lands and both gates pass on it,
  including spec QA accept rows 6 and 7.
- **M2. Spec QA accept row 2 is unmet AND unnamed in the deferral list: no
  bi-monthly and no semi-annual subscription exists anywhere in the scripted
  year.** The fixture (`02_fixture_s1.sql`) contains only monthly casts and
  one quarterly (CARL). Frequencies 2 and 6 exist as schema and data but were
  never billed, never covered, never spread in any executed proof. The proof
  document's deferral list names the console and the scenario sweep but not
  this. The follow-up increment must script at least one bi-monthly and one
  semi-annual subscription through billing, coverage, and spread.
- **M3. Spec QA accept row 3 is mostly unmet at this commit (disclosed):
  pause variants, early resume, auto-resume, pause-during-past_due,
  cancel-during-dunning, the 2017 and 2018 lanes, date change with the
  double-billing disclosure, day-28 truncation, and reactivation end to end
  were never driven in the simulated year.** The member-action functions
  exist (A13) but existence is not evidence of behavior. Day-28 truncation,
  "the mission's named case" in spec section 8.3, deserves special mention:
  no scenario exercises the empty-ladder rule directly (only the global G3
  clip invariant covers it indirectly), and it is likewise not named in the
  deferral list. Suspension, auto-cancel, and the day-31 clamp ARE proven
  (W-B, D1 to D3).

### LOW

- **L1. The proof document's "recorded output" and "recorded dates" blocks
  are condensed reformats, not verbatim transcript excerpts.** Every value in
  them traces exactly (the FM1, FM2 and catch-up blocks match the transcript
  line for line in content), but FRED's twelve dates are never PRINTED in the
  transcript; they are the expected string asserted inside battery D2, proven
  equal by the PASS. Presenting asserted-equal values as "recorded dates" is
  a hair of overstatement. Future proof documents should either quote the
  transcript verbatim or say "asserted equal by the battery".
- **L2. ROADMAP is stale: "SUBSCRIPTION ENGINE, spec in flight" describes a
  state two commits old.** The engine is built and locally proven. Update the
  ROADMAP entry when the phase state next changes (documentation-truth
  standing duty).
- **L3. Battery E1 asserts `count(*) >= 12` where the proof document claims
  exactly 13 pre-flight stops.** The printed count is 13 and traces the
  claim, but the assertion as written would also pass at 12. Tighten to
  equality in the next battery revision.
