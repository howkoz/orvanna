# Figma visual pack, MLM Pilot

As of 2026-08-15. Two boards now. The Whole Machine explains the BUSINESS, what a
member's money does and how the factory built it. System Flows and Design System
explains the BUILD, what the screens are and what they are made of. They are
companions, not replacements.

## The Whole Machine (end-to-end board)

- Board: https://www.figma.com/board/iZoo4SaTglllroNPLtAQiY
- Plain URL: https://www.figma.com/board/iZoo4SaTglllroNPLtAQiY
- What it shows: two columns. LEFT (blue) = the product machine, what a member's money
  does from subscribing to an AI agent through volumes, qualification, ranks, the
  commission run, the statement, the database, and the orvanna.io portal. RIGHT
  (violet) = the factory, Howard's decisions through the architect's rulebook, the
  database engineer's build, the two gates (verifier + quality assurance), the cloud
  ship, and the next phases. Numbered 1 to 16 across both columns; crossing violet
  arrows are the handoffs; the violet box at the bottom is the money punchline.
- Built with the /visual-explainer frozen format (manual placement, section-wrapped,
  screenshot-verified).

## System Flows and Design System (the build board)

Added 2026-08-15 by the designer, alongside `DOCUMENTATION\07-DESIGN-SYSTEM.md`.

- Board: https://www.figma.com/board/m4qgXgmSqjOjLTuhflVj5L
- Plain URL: https://www.figma.com/board/m4qgXgmSqjOjLTuhflVj5L
- What it shows, five sections:
  - `01  The whole system`: three lanes of user (visitor, member, staff), seven steps
    each, over one shared foundation of four stylesheets.
  - `02  Function, the shop and the checkout`: the seven step happy path, the bank
    approval branch (3-D Secure), and the six states the shopper watches change.
  - `03  Function, the member portal`: sign in, the Office landing, the five boards,
    the five tabs, and the theming note.
  - `04  Function, the staff call console`: six call steps, the three cards that keep
    the agent inside the rules, and the deliberate differences from the shop.
  - `05  The palette and the rhythm`: token swatches at their real hex values, the
    four light theme corrections of 2026-08-15, and the type and spacing scale.
- Also carries 28 real Figma colour variables in a collection called `Orvanna colour`,
  with `Dark` and `Light` modes. Switching the mode switches every bound colour, the
  same mechanism the real portal uses. No plugin needed.
- Built with manual placement and screenshot-verified, same discipline as the board
  above.
