1. The products: AI agents

Orvanna sells digital AI agents as monthly subscriptions. There is nothing to ship and nothing to stock. Each subscription bills monthly and carries Personal Volume (PV), the plan's point value. PV equals dollars, one for one.

TierWhat they doExamplesPrice / monthPV / month
Domain agentsrun one business function end to endPayment Agent, Shipping Agent, Pricing Agent, Inventory Agent, Marketing Agent, Tax Agent$100.00100
Support agentsstaff roles that assist the businessSoftware Engineer, Quality Assurance, Secretary, Chief Executive, Accounting, Customer Care$50.0050

Customers

Anyone can enjoy Orvanna agents WITHOUT joining the program, as a customer of one referring member. A customer subscribes at the same prices, and the full PV of every customer purchase counts toward the referring member's Sales Volume automatically: it helps that member qualify, feeds their upline's Team Volume, and is paid on by the upline like any other volume. Customers themselves never pay a joining fee, never hold a rank, and never earn commissions; their receipts always name them as the buyer.

3. Qualifying each month

The one gate: 100 PV. A month counts for you when your own SV is 100 or more. Exactly 100 qualifies. One domain agent does it, two support agents do it, any mix reaching 100 does it.

Being qualified does two things at once:

  1. You can be paid. Commissions for a month are paid only to members qualified in that month. An unqualified month means no check, with no exceptions and no makeup later.
  2. Your sponsor's leg count includes you. Rank requirements count ACTIVE legs, and your leg is active for your sponsor only when you are qualified.

A member holding a single support agent (50 PV) stays enrolled and keeps their team, but is not qualified: no check that month, and their sponsor loses that leg for rank purposes. Qualification resets every month; every month stands alone.

Two roads to 100. Your own subscriptions and your customers' subscriptions count the same. A member with zero personal spend and two customer-held support agents (2 times 50 PV) is fully qualified: qualification can rest entirely on volume you SELL, not volume you buy.

4. Ranks

Ranks recompute from scratch every month: you are what you did this month. You hold the HIGHEST rank whose requirements you fully meet. Every threshold is inclusive ("or more").

"A leg containing a Builder" means anyone in that leg, at any depth, earned Builder or higher this month.

5. How you get paid: level pay

Each month you earn a percentage of the CV of every member within your PAID DEPTH (set by your rank, table above). The percentages by level:

Level below you12345
Percent of that member's CV10%5%5%3%2%

Followed through one domain agent subscription: $100.00 of revenue is 100 PV, which is 80.00 CV. The five levels above the buyer can earn at most 8.00 + 4.00 + 4.00 + 2.40 + 1.60 = 20.00, that is 20 percent of revenue. In practice less is paid out, because:

  • an unqualified upline member earns nothing (their share is simply not paid);
  • an upline member whose paid depth stops short of the buyer's level is not paid for it;
  • there is NO compression: missed shares never roll to anyone else.

Every commission line is rounded half up to 2 decimals at the line level. Totals are sums of rounded lines. The buyer's OWN qualification never matters for the upline: all CV pays upward, whoever generated it.

7. The operational calendar

  • Volume month: every order is stamped with its volume month at creation, in Coordinated Universal Time (UTC). Subscription orders always stamp the 1st of the month. The stamp never moves afterward.
  • Cutoff: a calendar month's volume is complete when the month ends. Nothing enters a closed month.
  • The run: after month end, the commission run computes volumes, qualification, ranks, and every commission line for that month, then is marked FINAL.
  • Finality: a final run's statements are locked. They cannot be edited, deleted, or quietly added to.

8. Cancellations, refunds, and reruns

  • Cancelling a subscription stops its billing and its PV from the next month forward. The months already billed keep their volume.
  • Refunds: not part of this version. When a future version introduces refunds, the published rule will state exactly how volume and paid commissions claw back. Until then, no refund can occur in the demo.
  • Reruns: if a finalized month must ever be recomputed, the original run is preserved and marked superseded, and a NEW numbered run is issued. Statements always trace to the exact run that produced them.
