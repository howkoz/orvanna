The fix round's structural changes, in one paragraph: every schedule-shaping
action (pause, billing-day change, frequency change, reactivation) now
resolves a REBASE into its event row at action time (first re-based cycle
index, its billing date, its first covered month), and every later cycle
derives from that in lockstep, so the E3 transition rule holds to the
letter, paused months materialise as skipped_paused marker periods, and the
old shift arithmetic is gone. Attempt supersession is hygiene enforced by
trigger. The auto-cancel counter is a lane-aware backward walk
(fn_sub_unpaid_streak): paid coverage breaks the chain, T10/T5 pauses reset
it, T10a pauses freeze it, unpaid and suspension-gap months count. The
engine stamps a per-line covered_month_offset so the bridge books volume to
COVERED months (zero for every unbroken schedule and every shop line,
nothing else moves).

## What the gates should probe hardest, fix round

1. **The rebase derivation** is now the load-bearing novelty: one mechanism
   for pause, day change, frequency change and reactivation. Probe stacked
   rebases (pause after day-change: the bill-versus-covered offset must
   persist through the pause; a second day-change after a pause), and
   probe that pre-rebase cycles are exactly the frozen period rows.
2. **fn_sub_unpaid_streak**: the lane-aware walk. Adversarial sequences of
   alternating T10 and T10a pauses; a pause taken in the SAME month as an
   unpaid period; the 36-month walk cap.
3. **The OQ4 letter under multi-month frequencies**: PAULA is monthly; a
   quarterly day-change transition covers three uncovered months from one
   charge and the offset persists. Only arithmetic guarantees it; no
   scenario drives it yet.
4. **The T21 CIT bookkeeping period** (WALT): a paid period with no
   attempt and no bridged volume is a deliberate Path B artifact; in S2
   the real shop checkout replaces it and the bridge books its volume.
   Confirm the S2 test plan covers that seam.
5. **covered_month_offset in the bridge**: negative offsets are possible
   only via catch-up across a month boundary; the finalized-month trigger
   is the backstop. Worth one adversarial probe.
6. **Scale**: the gather loop remains a linear scan per tick (fine at
   pilot scale, an S2 optimization), and fn_sub_scheduled_date now does
   per-call event lookups; the cycle audit view is the heaviest reader.
