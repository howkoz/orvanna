## 2. The five requested rulings

### 2a. D6, the anchor deviation: BUILDER CORRECT

The spec is internally inconsistent and the builder resolved it the only
defensible way. Ruling R1 says the default first billing is "30 days after
the initial purchase"; spec 4.2 prose says "The first renewal bills on that
date" (the anchor) and its example (purchase 2026-01-05, anchor 2026-02-04,
"monthly renewals on the 4th thereafter") makes renewal 1 the anchor date.
The 4.2 sketch formula `n * frequency_months` contradicts all of that: it
would put renewal 1 one full period after the anchor, and it would break
spec 4.3's coverage continuity, because the initial checkout covers the
purchase month as period zero and the anchor month would then be covered by
nothing. The builder's `(n - 1) * frequency_months` is the only reading under
which R1, the example, and continuous coverage all hold. Probed live:
`fn_scheduled_date(2026-02-04, null, 0) = 2026-02-04` and `(..., 1) =
2026-03-04`. The spec's sketch formula should be corrected by the architect
(spec errata, not a code change). Every date in the build stands.

### 2b. Adversarial pause timing and the auto-cancel derivation (12.7, OQ2)

The auto-cancel sweep itself held every probe:

- Worked example B reproduced (suspended Sep 26, cancelled Nov 1, T22).
- A hard-declined member (card_update_required Oct 1, suspended at the
  Oct 26 checkpoint via T17) auto-cancels at Dec 1 with October and November
  unpaid: correct month-start arithmetic, third-month start.
- Pause then resume then immediate fail: a member who paused October,
  auto-resumed Nov 4, then failed the whole December ladder and suspended,
  cancelled at Feb 1 on December plus January. The pause window BEFORE the
  counted months does not block ruling R4. Correct.
- Pause in month two of unpaid: refused, because a suspended subscription
  cannot pause (no transition lane), which matches table 5.2. Probed, raises
  cleanly.
- OQ2 reset window: implemented as "no pause event whose pause_until reaches
  into the two counted months". Boundary behavior: a pause ending exactly on
  the first counted month's first day defers cancellation by one month.
  Member-favorable, at most one month, consistent with a defensible reading
  of "the counter resets at engagement". LOW note L4, acceptable.

But the pause FUNCTION feeding these scenarios is broken: see HIGH-1.

### 2c. Early-resume refusal inside the first paused month: ACCEPTABLE FOR S1, RULING REQUIRED BEFORE S3

Two narrowings versus spec 12.2's "an early resume re-derives the schedule
with the actual months skipped": (1) resume inside the first paused month
raises an exception (probed; loud, nothing corrupted); (2) a two-month pause
resumed after one month keeps the full two-month shift, so the schedule is
not re-derived with the actual skip. The builder states the limitation
honestly inside the function and in the evidence. Because S1 has no member
surface, refusal is loud rather than silently wrong, and no money or volume
is misbooked, this is MEDIUM spec drift (M4), not a gate-stopper, on the
condition that Howard rules on it before any Phase S3 member surface exposes
resume. If Howard wants true partial-resume, the event schema needs a
representation for sub-month or negative shifts, which is a design change,
not a patch.

### 2d. D8, internal_config routing: ACCEPTABLE AS BUILT

The spec's own words in 6.2 table 5 allow either route ("routed to
card_update_required or the staff attention queue"), and the spec's
governing member-fault versus system-fault principle argues for the queue:
moving a member to card_update_required for our own defect would punish the
member and solicit a needless card update. Probed: the corrupted-credential
member stayed active all year, zero retries, zero member-clock movement,
thirteen attention-queue rows, and the re-attempt-monthly-forever behavior
is visible to staff each month. Two consequences to carry forward, neither
gating: the affected renewal_periods stay outcome 'open' indefinitely
(ledger accumulation, see L1), and there is no escalation rule after N
system-fault months (the builder's own note; a future data-shaped rule).

### 2e. The FM1 idempotency key, attacked: HOLDS ON EVERY REACHABLE PATH; ONE UNGUARDED COLUMN IS A LATENT BREAK

- Double tick, crash rerun, catch-up after a skipped day: zero duplicates
  (reproduced, plus the year-wide sweep: no subscription has any covered
  calendar month paid twice).
- Billing-day change mid-cycle: cannot mint two billings for one cycle; the
  month component of every cycle is fixed by anchor arithmetic and only the
  day moves, so coverage neither gaps nor doubles (probed; see M1 for the
  separate timing deviation).
- Reactivation re-anchor: indexes continue monotonically, the post-reanchor
  branch prices the next cycle one full period after the new anchor, no
  collision, cycle audit clean (probed end to end through a paid
  post-reactivation cycle).
- Frequency change: THE HOLE. No function exists to change
  `frequency_months`, and no guard refuses a raw UPDATE. Probed: switching a
  quarterly subscription to monthly re-derives every cycle date under the
  new frequency, and the next tick catch-up bills months the quarterly
  billing already covered: four duplicate charges minted, one calendar month
  booked 500.00 SV against a correct 100.00. The FM1 key is per
  (subscription_id, renewal_index), not per covered month, so it cannot stop
  this. Unreachable through any shipped surface today, therefore MEDIUM
  (M2) rather than HIGH, but the spec 9A.2 console promises a
  frequency-change control, so semantics plus a guard must exist before that
  control ships.

## 3. Findings

### HIGH

**H1. `fn_sub_pause` is broken for T10 in the common case; the evidence
overclaims it as proven.** Mechanism, probed live: when a billing attempt
declines and a retry later executes, the superseded attempt's `next_action`
stays 'retry' forever (the tick's gather protects itself by reading only the
latest attempt; nothing ever clears the stale rows). `fn_sub_pause` iterates
EVERY attempt with `next_action in ('retry','infra_immediate')` and inserts
a skipped_clipped row at `attempt_no + 1` for each, colliding with the
already-existing next attempt: `duplicate key value violates unique
constraint "billing_attempts_renewal_period_id_attempt_no_key"`. Result: a
member in past_due can pause ONLY before the first retry has run (probed:
day-of-decline pause works; pause after one executed retry always throws).
Ruling R3 and spec 12.3 promise pause during past_due, period. The proof
year never exercised pause, resume, cancel, billing-day change,
reactivation, or the 2017/2018 lanes, yet the evidence document's "Built
and proven" paragraph names them as proven; the deferral list only deferred
the CONSOLE, not the functions' proof. Wrong result on a ruled behavior
plus an overclaim in evidence: HIGH. Fix shape (builder's choice, verifier
fixes nothing): clear or supersede `next_action` on prior attempts when a
successor attempt is written, or filter the pause loop to the latest attempt
per period, and add pause-mid-ladder (plus the other member actions) to the
scripted proof year so the battery covers them.

### MEDIUM

**M1. Billing-day change deviates from the accepted OQ4 transition rule and
records a disclosure that does not match its own behavior.** Spec 12.1 and
OQ4 (accepted as written): the transition charge runs on the FIRST
occurrence of the new billing day strictly after the change, so the change
month may carry two billings, and coverage stays continuous. Implementation:
the next cycle simply adopts the new day inside its own anchor month.
Probed: day-1 subscription changed to day 25 on 2028-02-02; spec's letter
says a charge on 2028-02-25; the engine billed 2028-03-25. Coverage never
gaps and never doubles (probed), no month is double-billed, and the member
is charged later, never earlier; but the accepted ruling's letter is not
implemented, the deviation is unflagged (it appears in no deviation list),
and the `billing_day_change` event text written by the function says "the
change month may contain two billings, exactly once", which is FALSE of the
behavior the function produces. Either implement OQ4's letter or take the
implemented rule back to Howard and correct the event text the same day.

**M2. `frequency_months` is unguarded and re-derives history.** Section 2e
above. A raw UPDATE mints duplicate charges for already-covered months on
the very next tick. Latent today (no shipped surface changes it); must be
closed with a guard trigger, or with defined re-indexing semantics, before
the spec 9A.2 console's frequency-change control exists.

**M3. Spec conflict on pause-during-dunning, resolved silently in the
restrictive direction.** Spec 12.3's heading and first word accept pause
"during past_due or dunning" (T10); table 5.2 defines no dunning-to-paused
lane; the trigger and `fn_sub_pause` refuse dunning (probed). A member nine
days into a failing ladder is in dunning, so the refusal bites real members.
The builder chose the table without flagging the contradiction. Needs an
architect errata and Howard's call: either add a dunning-to-paused lane
(T10a) or amend 12.3.

**M4. Early resume narrowed versus 12.2.** Section 2c above. Flagged
honestly by the builder; requires Howard's ruling before Phase S3.

**M5. Frequencies 2 and 6 are never exercised.** The scripted year bills
monthly and quarterly only; no bi-monthly or semi-annual subscription ever
billed in any proof, so their spread slices (2 and 6), coverage windows and
auto-cancel interaction ride solely on parameterized arithmetic. Spec 14's
QA gate item 2 requires each frequency demonstrated. Belongs to the QA
completeness gate, recorded here because the correctness re-run after H1
should add both cheaply.

### LOW

**L1. Dead outcome values.** `renewal_periods.outcome` values
'skipped_paused' and 'void_cancelled' are never written by any code path
(year-plus-probes census: open, paid, unpaid only). Pauses defer cycles
instead of materialising skipped periods, so the auto-cancel sweep's
'skipped_paused' excusal branch is inert (the pause_until window does all
excusing), and `fn_sub_cancel` marks open periods 'unpaid' even when
cancellation is unrelated to payment failure, where the spec's shape
intended 'void_cancelled'. Cosmetic today; misleading to future readers of
the ledger.

**L2. Stale `next_action` on superseded attempts.** The data condition
underlying H1 (probed: all five superseded attempts of a fully-laddered
period still read 'retry'). Any future consumer (the console's retry queue,
spec 9A.2) will overcount unless it filters to the latest attempt per
period.

**L3. Same-state self-transitions are allowed for all seven states.** The
guard permits any state to "transition" to itself; the spec lists only T1
(active to active) as the recorded no-op. Harmless idempotency, one line of
intent worth a comment.

**L4. OQ2 boundary grace.** Section 2b: a pause ending exactly on the first
counted month's boundary defers auto-cancel by one month, member-favorable.
