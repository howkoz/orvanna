02

You are not selling software

Nobody wakes up wanting to buy software. What people want is for a specific, hated, recurring job to stop being theirs. A Conductor who understands that outsells one who has memorised the catalog.

AN ILLUSTRATION, NOT A CUSTOMER STORY

A plumbing company with two vans

The owner quotes all day and invoices at night. Three invoices a month go unpaid until the bank balance makes it urgent. Every quarter the tax return happens in a panic across a weekend, from a shoebox of receipts.

WEAK Opens the catalog and starts at Payment Agent.
GOOD Asks which of those three things they would pay to never think about again — and listens. If the answer is the quarterly weekend, the answer is the Tax Agent, on its own, and nothing else this month.

One agent. Set up in an afternoon. That is the whole sale — the second and third come later, after the owner has watched the first one work and starts naming the next thing they hate.

WHY CONDUCTOR, NOT DISTRIBUTOR

Most direct-selling companies borrow their word from a warehouse: distributor, dealer, representative. Those words fit when the thing being moved is a box. Orvanna does not ship boxes — its products go to work inside somebody else's business, and the person who sold them is still needed the day after the sale.

You do not playThe agents do the bookkeeping and chase the declined cards. Your hands stay off the instruments, which is what makes the work scale past your own hours.
You choose the scoreA florist and a freight broker do not need the same agents. Picking the right two or three, in the right order, is the part software cannot do for itself.
You keep timeThe ensemble that fit a shop in March is short a player by September, when it starts selling into a second country and has filings it has never done before.
