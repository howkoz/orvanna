REVIEWorvanna.io — what I would change

Read from the four screens you sent plus the repo. The brand voice is strong and the copy is unusually good — the problems are structural: three live layout bugs, a lot of dead vertical space, and a catalog that gives a visitor no place to start. Fix the bugs first; the rest is hierarchy.

  1. 01

    Overlapping content, two places. On the home page the “One platform underneath” cards collide with the team paragraph; on checkout the “Billing address” heading sits under the “Full name” label, and step 3 (Activation) is missing from the visible sequence — the steps read 1, 2, 4.Both look like absolutely-positioned or negative-margin sections rather than a flow issue. Nothing cosmetic matters until these are gone.

  2. 02

    The closing section renders at roughly a third opacity — a scroll reveal that never fired.Author reveals as opacity 1 by default and let the observer remove a class, so a missed intersection fails visible instead of invisible.

  3. 03

    The PV meter reads “800 / 100 PV” with a bar past its own track.Cap the fill at 100%, and once it is met switch the label to “Qualified month met · 800 PV” — a ratio above one reads as a bug, not an achievement.

  4. 04

    Body copy is 13.4px at #C7D0DE on near-black; nav and labels are 0.84rem uppercase at 0.14em.The whole site reads like fine print. 16–17px body, tighter tracking on labels, and reserve uppercase for kickers.

  5. 05

    300–500px of empty page between sections, and the story section uses only the left half of an 1100px column.Either give the right column something real — the two gates, a build timeline, the one-rule pull quote — or close the gap. Empty space that is not composed reads as unfinished.

  6. 06

    The hero headline drops “CLOCK” onto its own line in a different color.Set a controlled measure with an intentional break, and stop tinting the last word.

  7. 07

    The catalog is thirteen identical cards at two identical prices with the same full-width button.Nothing signals where to start, what differs, or what a pack saves. Promote one recommended pack, make the twelve agents a dense scannable list, and keep the PV maths on screen while shopping.

  8. 08

    Eleven nav controls wrap and centre-pack below 560px with no disclosure.A real menu button under 900px; the cart and Sign In stay in the bar.

  9. 09

    The order summary is a scroll region nested inside the page scroll.At the moment of payment, never hide totals behind a second scrollbar — let the rail grow and stick.

  10. 10

    Every block is a rounded card with a gradient top border, in two different gradients, and every agent wears the same hexagon.One accent, square corners, rules instead of boxes — and give each agent a distinct mark so the catalog is scannable by shape.
